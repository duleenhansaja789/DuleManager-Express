5. SHARED GROUPS, BILLS, REPAYMENTS AND ARCHIVES
Invite only people who should review the group's shared information. Current members can see shared purchases, exact spending shares, corrections, bill schedules and repayment activity, including cancellation or dispute reasons. Group owners manage membership and invitations. Resolve disagreements about participation, amounts and payments directly with the people involved; the developer does not arbitrate or collect debts.
Review equal, custom and percentage splits before saving. Custom amounts must equal the purchase total, and entered percentages must total 100. Percentage allocations are rounded into exact currency units while preserving the total; saved shares are the amounts used in balances. When correcting an existing split, review the exact amounts shown.
Shared bill schedules are plans. A due-date notification or schedule does not create spending, prove that money is owed or automatically transfer money. A member must record an actual paid occurrence. Review the date, payer, amount and participants; do not record the same payment again as an unrelated purchase. When a schedule changes, review future occurrences and any historical entries separately.
Recorded repayments do not send money. Only the recipient may confirm receipt or dispute a pending repayment; only the payer may cancel their pending request. Confirmation, cancellation and dispute compete for one final request status. A closed request cannot be overwritten by another decision. Cancelled and disputed requests do not change balances, and closing a request does not reverse an actual transfer. Discuss disputed details and, where appropriate, create a new accurate request. A confirmation is a member's acknowledgment, not independent bank verification. Do not confirm money you have not received.
Corrections, cancellation reasons and disputes remain in history. Leaving a group does not remove its history from remaining members or erase information already seen or exported. Transfer ownership or leave groups before deleting a personal app account. A sole remaining owner may explicitly delete the group's history and bill schedules.
A semester archive is read-only storage, not deletion or settlement. The owner may reopen it to resume activity or settle balances. Archived pages show partial history without calculating partial balances; a complete CSV report reads the full requested history, subject to the 20,000-event export limit. Exported reports are not password protected or a restorable shared-group backup. Protect other members' information when saving or sharing them.
Importing a shared purchase into your private budget requires confirmation. Review corrected imports when prompted. Check your share, the amount you paid and the selected wallet; avoid duplicating the same personal cost. Shared repayments and private wallet movements must be reviewed separately.

6. SYNCHRONIZATION, BACKUPS AND RECOVERY
Keep copies of information you need. A cloud restore can recover only information successfully synchronized with the same app account. Signing out, uninstalling, clearing storage, losing your device or changing accounts can make unsynchronized information unavailable.
Free-plan quotas, network failures and service interruptions may delay cloud synchronization. Review the app's saving status. If conflicting edits are preserved, review them before deciding which version to keep.
Plain JSON backups, Excel reports and shared-history CSV exports are not password protected by the app. Password-protected backups require the password you choose; the developer cannot recover it. Review a restore preview before importing. Existing record identifiers are kept when merging a backup.
