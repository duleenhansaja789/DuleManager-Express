Delete your DuleManager Express account

You can request deletion without installing or opening the app.

Request by email

Email dulemexpresshq@gmail.com with the subject “DuleManager Express account deletion”. Identify the Google email used for your app account and say whether you want to delete your account and its associated data. Send from that address when possible. Do not include your password, identity documents, budget details or backup files.

We may verify account ownership before processing the request. If you cannot access the original email, contact support to discuss verification. This request deletes your DuleManager Express app account, not your Google account.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to remove.
  2. Leave any live roommate groups. Transfer group ownership first, or delete a group if you are its sole remaining owner.
  3. Open Settings → Account & backup → Delete Google account and cloud data.
  4. Read the confirmation and complete deletion while online. Recent Google authentication may be required.

What is removed

Completed app-account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication app account and its personal cloud records, including synchronized expenses, allowances, goals and supported profile details. The in-app flow also removes the local data for that signed-in app profile.

Shared group records retained by other members, other people's copies, independently exported reports/backups and a separate guest notebook are not removed by personal account deletion. Shared history, bill schedules and semester archives remain until the relevant group is deleted. Archiving alone does not delete data. Account deletion clears the local account profile and its saved reminder metadata when completed. Locally exported files remain until their owners delete them. If a request cannot be completed, contact support rather than assuming deletion succeeded.

Guest records

Guest mode does not create a cloud account. To remove guest records on your device, use Android Settings → Apps → DuleManager Express → Storage → Clear storage. This also removes local app data; make any desired backup first.
